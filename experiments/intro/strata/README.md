# Intro variant E: Strata

Ported 2026-10-16 from branch `intro/strata` (tip of 2026-09-27) into the per-experiment contract: `_partials/`, `scss/`, `js/` and `_data/experiments/intro/strata/` are this design's only sources; `/assets` media and the `_config.yml` author block are shared. The branch keeps the full build report and iteration history.

## Concept

1. The intro is a cross-section of the stone: three pillar courses (Hardware, Software, People) laid as horizontal beds the full width of the composition, and the portrait as a lens cut through them.
2. Act 1: Hardware and Software are two courses above the lens, People the base course below it; the lens is a chamfered octagon (45 degree cuts, the stone's chamfer language) and the courses beside it are cut to receive it, meeting on one seam at the lens's midline.
3. Act 2: the courses shear sideways: each one's left run is cut off as its copy slides right into the right column, and the notches around the lens close flat; the lens then slides left into the cleared column and opens top and bottom into the fitted portrait panel; People rises under Software; the Forged panel is cut down from the base course.
4. Settled: portrait panel left, three courses stacked right (label beside line), Forged panel (tagline, blurb, link) under them; one fitted slab.
5. Every piece carries its own cut-edge hairline (an even-odd ring clip that morphs with the glass), so no piece is ever a soft rectangle; nothing moves across the lens, and the portrait is stacked above every course.
