# Intro variant G: Shard

Ported 2026-10-16 from branch `intro/shard` (tip of 2026-09-27) into the per-experiment contract: `_partials/`, `scss/`, `js/` and `_data/experiments/intro/shard/` are this design's only sources; `/assets` media and the `_config.yml` author block are shared. The branch keeps the full build report and iteration history.

## Concept

1. One vein cuts the stage on the slab's rising diagonal; the portrait is set in the cut (the vein runs behind it) and its right edge is cut exactly perpendicular to the vein.
2. The three pillars are shards lying on the vein, rotated to its angle, square ends butted against the portrait's edge: Hardware and Software end to end above the vein, People below it, centred under their joint (two on one side, one on the other; nothing stranded in a corner).
3. Act 2: the vein draws back into the point where it meets the portrait; the shards straighten to level and rise into one row beside the portrait (Hardware and Software almost in place, People runs along under them and rises into the third cell); the portrait slides to its column. No piece is ever left of the portrait's right edge, so nothing crosses the face.
4. The vein grows back out of that point along the portrait's edge as the seam, and the Forged stone is cut open from the seam across, under the row; the shards' glass fuses into it and the tagline, blurb and link rise onto it.
5. Settled: portrait left and panel right, fitted along one leaning seam (the vein turned a quarter); one lit line between them, the portrait's and panel's inlay rings open on the seam side so the joint is a single vein.
