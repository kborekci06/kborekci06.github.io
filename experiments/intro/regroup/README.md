# Intro variant D: Regroup

Ported 2026-10-16 from branch `intro/regroup` (tip of 2026-09-27) into the per-experiment contract: `_partials/`, `scss/`, `js/` and `_data/experiments/intro/regroup/` are this design's only sources; `/assets` media and the `_config.yml` author block are shared. The branch keeps the full build report and iteration history.

## Concept

1. Act 1, assemble: the face is the centre stone; Hardware arrives from the left, Software from the right, People rises from below, and the three lock around it as one composite (three equal stones, each touching the face along one full edge; the joints light as they lock). Nothing else is on the stage.
2. The composite is symmetric about the face: Hardware | face | Software share one base line, People sits centred under the face; the face stands proud of the two side stones (C's keystone read, without a fourth stone).
3. Act 2, regroup: scrolling on, the face slides left into its fitted panel and the three stones travel into one row beside it; each stone's cut changes from its act-1 outline to its place in B's panel.
4. Only then does the "Forged through craft and legacy." stone form: its glass is cut down from the row's lower edge, the seams and both inlay rings draw on, and the tagline, blurb and link rise onto it.
5. Settled = B: portrait left as a fitted panel, pillars as a row on the right, the Forged panel fitted under them; every pillar line readable. JS off and reduced motion show exactly this.
