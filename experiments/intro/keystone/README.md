Ported 2026-10-16 from branch `intro/keystone` (tip of 2026-09-27) into the per-experiment contract: `_partials/`, `scss/`, `js/` and `_data/experiments/intro/keystone/` are this design's only sources; `/assets` media and the `_config.yml` author block are shared. The branch keeps the full build report and iteration history.

## Concept

1. The portrait is the keystone of an arch: a tapered stone (wider at the top, standing proud above the course at desktop) set in the middle of the top course, with its own cut edge and inlay hairline.
2. Hardware arrives from the left and Software from the right as the two voussoirs whose inner edges are cut to the keystone's taper; People arrives from below as the base course that carries the tagline, the blurb and the link.
3. The keystone is set last: it drops into the slot the three facets leave, the way a mason locks an arch (at 390 the stack is portrait first, so there it drops first and the facets lock under its point).
4. Once it sits, the composite's outer inlay ring draws on, broken only by the keystone standing proud, and the course seam draws along the base.
5. Settled, one fitted stone: four pieces, slanted and pointed joints, one ring; the face is clear of every line, glass and sheen.
