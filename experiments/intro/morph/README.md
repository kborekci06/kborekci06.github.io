# Intro variant B: Morph

Ported 2026-10-16 from branch `intro/morph` (tip of 2026-09-27) into the per-experiment contract: `_partials/`, `scss/`, `js/` and `_data/experiments/intro/morph/` are this design's only sources; `/assets` media and the `_config.yml` author block are shared. The branch keeps the full build report and iteration history.

## Concept

1. The trifecta arrives as now: three glass facets (Hardware, Software, People) apart and tilted, joining into one row that spans the whole slab.
2. The face is already there, under the first facet: the portrait panel sits in the slab's first column, closed to nothing.
3. Scrolling on, the trifecta slides off the portrait as one piece (right from 1024 px, down when stacked), each facet narrowing as it goes, and the portrait opens behind the trailing edge of the first facet: the clip edge is the facet's edge, so the facets uncover the face, it never fades in.
4. The three facets close into the joined facet ("Forged through craft and legacy.", blurb, link); the portrait's inlay and the joined facet's inlay draw on, and the face settles (scale 1.12 to 1).
5. Settled: two fitted pieces of one stone, portrait left with the wide lower-outer cut, joined facet right with its mirror cut, butt-jointed; stacked below 1024 px, portrait on top of the joined facet.
