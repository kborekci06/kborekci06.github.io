# I build medical devices and intelligent systems for healthcare

I’m an M.S. Mechanical Engineering student (B.S. Biomedical Engineering), focused on **embedded systems, signal processing, and machine learning** that translate to the clinic. My work spans a **patent-pending diaphragm control device** and a **pose-lifting evaluation pipeline** for infant motor assessment—both built with a bias toward reliability, reproducibility, and clinical relevance.

- **Embedded & Control:** RP2040/SAM4S, embedded C, multicore tasking, I2C/UART/SPI, SD logging @ **100 Hz**.  
- **Data & ML:** signal processing, feature engineering, **MMPose / PyTorch**, CUDA where it matters.  
- **Translational rigor:** **Patent pending**, **BMES 2025** selection, IRB-approved trials; coauthor on **Nature Digital Medicine**.
