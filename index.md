# I build medical devices and intelligent systems for healthcare

I’m an M.S. Mechanical Engineering student (B.S. Biomedical Engineering) who designs **embedded systems, signal processing, and ML pipelines** for real clinical use. Recent work spans a **patent-pending diaphragm control device** and a **pose-lifting evaluation pipeline** for infant motor assessment—built for reliability, reproducibility, and clinician impact.
