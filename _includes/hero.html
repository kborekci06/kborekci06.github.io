Hello, I’m
Kaan Borekci

A Multidisciplinary engineer with a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering and M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Northwestern University. My background spans a wide variety of engineering fields, from mechanics, transport phenomena, and quantitative systems physiology through academics, to embedded systems and medical devices through hands-on product development, to applied clinical AI/computer vision through my work at the Shirley Ryan AbilityLab for the past 2+ years.

I pursue R&D and translational engineering roles at the boundary where disciplines intersect, where the ability to draw on multiple engineering disciplines simultaneously is what produces ideas that a single-domain approach would miss. I have taken products from concept through patent filing, IRB approval, and clinical trials, co-authored a Nature Digital Medicine publication, and built computational pipelines processing hundreds of thousands of clinical video frames.

My mission is to work in interface roles within MedTech, robotics, and defense/aerospace where I can bridge engineering teams, clinicians, patients, and business stakeholders to prototype and develop engineering solutions that improve quality of life for all.
